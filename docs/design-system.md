# Design system — Limewash Gallery: Bindi edition

> Source of truth for the storefront and admin look & feel. Tokens are implemented in `src/app/globals.css` (@theme). Shared brand components live in `src/components/brand.tsx`.

## Concept

Kavita's Artistry is presented as a small contemporary Indian gallery. The walls are warm limewash, every piece sits on the same pale stone plinth, and labels read like museum wall cards ('N° 014 · LIPPAN · KUTCH · 30 CM'). The interface is about 90% warm neutrals and 8% warm near-black ink. Sindoor vermilion stays under 3% and appears only as a bindi: the full stop of the wordmark, the eyebrow dot, the sliding active-tab dot, the focus ring and the free-shipping fill. That makes it at once the gallery's red 'sold' dot, kumkum and a Lippan mirror. Large light Fraunces headlines with one italic word, precise Manrope UI type, 1px hairlines instead of shadows, a jharokha arch in exactly two places and real craft facts (hours, mirror counts, region) create the premium feel. Ornament, gold, navy and script fonts are gone. Teal Lippan plates, mustard mandalas and multicolour pooja decor are always the most vivid things on screen. Each page ends with a giant edge-to-edge wordmark on a kajal-dark footer.

## Why this direction

Limewash Gallery wins on the two criteria that matter most for this catalogue: premium impression and product showcase. Kavita's pieces are already loud, so the only defensible strategy is a neutral wall: ink for actions, one sindoor dot for intent, and the products as the only saturated things on screen. Sindoor Studio is more memorable, but painting CTAs and a full band in vermilion fights wine, terracotta and kumkum-red pieces and drifts toward sale-energy. Mitti & Sindoor is a near-duplicate of the winner with internal contradictions.

Grafted from Sindoor Studio:
- The bindi as the brand atom: the wordmark full stop, eyebrow dots, and a single sliding bindi in the mobile tab bar, the signature micro-interaction.
- The jharokha arch mask, used in exactly two places: the hero image and Kavita's portrait.
- The giant edge-to-edge footer wordmark as the sign-off.
- The floating wall-label card on the hero.
- The size-ruler overlay on the scale shot.
- The 'Sold' state that desaturates the image and offers 'Commission a similar piece'.
- A once-per-hover mirror glint, limited to mirror-work products.

Grafted from Mitti & Sindoor:
- Manrope instead of Geist and Geist Mono. It has the ₹ glyph, tabular figures and a warmer humanist geometry, it is one fewer font file, and labels never go below 11px.
- Tiro Devanagari (preload:false) for one Hindi word per page.
- Small 16px icons on the trust row, because COD buyers scan for them.
- The 'neel' (indigo-dye) naming for the admin AI tone.

Fixes:
- The mobile hero image is capped at min(52svh, 440px) inside the arch, so the headline and CTA sit above the fold on 360px phones.
- The tab bar is de-duplicated to Home, Shop, Custom, Wishlist, Bag, with Search in the header.
- Success is darkened to #2B7049 and warning to #8C5A10 so both pass on plinth.
- Admin input borders become #8C8279 (3.76:1) and the admin placeholder becomes muted #645D54.
- The favicon is a bindi monogram at small sizes, with the medallion kept for 180/512px icons.
- One dark content band plus the footer, never adjacent.
- One plinth band per page.
- A seasonal festive mode swaps only on-dark accents to haldi.
- Every text pairing was recomputed with the sRGB relative-luminance formula, and all pass AA.

## Fonts

- **Display:** Fraunces (Google, variable, has ₹). In app/layout.tsx: Fraunces({ subsets:['latin'], axes:['opsz'], style:['normal','italic'], variable:'--font-fraunces', display:'swap' }), with NO weight array. axes:['opsz'] is mandatory, or the stubby 9pt cut is served. Do not load SOFT. Set font-optical-sizing:auto. Keep WONK at its default for italics (the hand-made lean on the one italic word). If roman h/n/m lean visibly at 64px+, add 'WONK' to axes and set .font-display{font-variation-settings:'WONK' 0} while leaving .italic wonky. Use it for headlines, product titles (17px minimum), the wordmark, pull quotes, step numerals and the admin greeting and page titles.
- **Body/UI:** Manrope (Google, variable 200–800, has ₹ U+20B9 in latin-ext, true tabular figures). Manrope({ subsets:['latin','latin-ext'], variable:'--font-manrope', display:'swap' }). Preload latin-ext because prices are above the fold. Use it for ALL functional text: nav, buttons, prices, forms, eyebrows/wall labels (uppercase tracked), badges, tab labels, and the whole admin.
- **Accent:** Tiro Devanagari Hindi, optional: Tiro_Devanagari_Hindi({ weight:'400', subsets:['devanagari'], preload:false, variable:'--font-deva' }). It is unicode-range gated, so it downloads only on pages that contain Hindi. At most one word per page: 'हस्तनिर्मित' in the hero eyebrow and footer, 'शुभ लाभ' on name-plate pages, seasonal 'शुभ दीपावली'. No script font anywhere. Kavita's real signature is an inline SVG.
- **Weights:** Fraunces: 340 for display, 360 for H1, 380 for H2, 400 for H3, product titles and quotes, and the wordmark; italic 340–380. Manrope: 400 for body, 500 for nav, prices, buttons and inputs, 600 for eyebrows, labels, badges and admin card titles, 700 for admin KPI values and the mobile large title.
- **Rules:** In globals.css: @theme inline { --font-display: var(--font-fraunces), ui-serif, Georgia, serif; --font-sans: var(--font-manrope), ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif; --font-deva: var(--font-deva), var(--font-fraunces), serif; }. Delete --font-script and remove Cormorant Garamond, Jost (no ₹ glyph) and Great Vibes from layout.tsx. Rules: (1) Serif only at 17px and up (product titles) and 22px and up elsewhere, never bold, never uppercase. (2) Exactly one italic word or phrase per headline ('Art made *slowly*, by hand.'). (3) Prices are never in serif: always Manrope 500 with font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums, formatted by a shared formatINR() = new Intl.NumberFormat('en-IN',{style:'currency',currency:'INR',maximumFractionDigits:0}) → ₹1,850 / ₹1,25,000. Admin compact uses notation:'compact' → ₹1.3L. (4) Headings get text-wrap:balance, paragraphs get text-wrap:pretty, and quotes get hanging-punctuation:first. (5) Use at most 4 type sizes per screen plus the display size. (6) Labels are never smaller than 11px, for budget 720p screens. (7) Wall labels use 'N°' (U+2116) and the middle dot ' · '.

## Layout

- Container: max-width 1440px, margin-inline auto, padding-inline 16px (<640) / 24px (≥640) / 40px (≥1024) / 64px (≥1440). Prose is capped at 62ch. Full-bleed bands break out of the container, and their content returns to it.
- Grid: 4 cols with a 12px gap on mobile, 8 cols with 20px at md, 12 cols with 24px at lg. Editorial modules are asymmetric: image in cols 1–7, text in cols 9–12 offset 96px down. Content is left-aligned by default.
- Spacing (4px base): 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160. Section padding-block is clamp(4rem, 2rem + 6vw, 8rem), i.e. 64→128px. The section-header-to-content gap is 32px mobile / 48px desktop. Inside cards: 12px from image to text, 4–6px between text rows.
- Band budget per page: canvas by default, plus at most ONE plinth band, plus at most ONE kajal (ink-deep) content band, plus the kajal footer. The kajal content band must never touch the footer; at least one canvas section sits between them. Never use two tinted bands in a row, and never colour sections by category.
- Section header: a full-width 1px line rule on top. 24px below it, a row: on the left the eyebrow (bindi + number), then the H2 12px under it; on the right a 'View all →' text link baseline-aligned to the H2. Below 768px the link moves under the H2.
- Radius tokens: --radius-img 2px (all images, tiles, thumbnails, quick view); --radius-ctl 4px (buttons, inputs, selects, steppers, storefront toasts with multiple lines); --radius-chip 999px (chips, badges, counters, tab dot, short toasts); --radius-sheet 20px (top corners of mobile sheets); --radius-arch 999px 999px 2px 2px (ONLY the hero image and the Meet Kavita portrait); --radius-card-admin 8px; --radius-ctl-admin 6px. Do not use rounded-2xl or rounded-3xl anywhere.
- Borders: 1px only. Use --color-line for structure, --color-line-strong for control outlines, and 1px ink for selected chips and secondary buttons.
- Shadows: none on in-flow elements. Floating layers (drawer, quick view, search panel, mega menu, popovers, toasts, sheets) use --shadow-float: 0 1px 2px rgb(28 25 22 / .04), 0 24px 60px -20px rgb(28 25 22 / .25). Elsewhere, elevation comes from a surface shift plus a hairline.
- Product grid: 2 cols (gap-x 12, gap-y 28) <768; 3 cols (20/40) ≥768; 4 cols (24/48) ≥1280. On Shop, insert one 2-column editorial tile at position 8 and every 12 after it (a craft story on plinth, a 'Commission a piece' prompt, or a quote on kajal). It must never be the first row on mobile.
- Aspect ratios: product cards and PDP 4:5; category tiles 3:4; editorial 3:2 or 4:5; hero 4:5 inside an arch; UGC 1:1. Round and flat wall pieces (Lippan plates, mandalas, wall plates, name plates; flag product.fit='contain') use object-contain with 8% padding on plinth. Lifestyle shots use object-cover. White-background cut-outs get mix-blend-mode:multiply, except white or cream products.
- Breakpoints: sm 640, md 768, lg 1024, xl 1280, 2xl 1440. The storefront tab bar shows below lg. Below lg, <main> gets padding-bottom calc(64px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 16px).
- Z-index: header 40, tab bar 40, sticky buy bar 45, scrim 50, drawer/sheet 60, search overlay 70, toast 80.
- Touch targets are at least 44×44px with 8px separation. Focus: :focus-visible{outline:2px solid var(--color-accent);outline-offset:2px}, using --color-accent-on-dark on dark surfaces. Never remove the outline without a replacement.
- Tailwind v4: replace the existing navy/cream/gold values in the globals.css @theme block in place, keeping the semantic token names already used (canvas, tint→plinth alias, ink, accent, muted, admin-*). Add --ease-*/--dur-* tokens and --shadow-float. Keep the old names as aliases (e.g. --color-tint: var(--color-plinth)) for one pass so existing classes don't break.

## Motion

- Principle: short, precise, quiet. UI motion stays under 300ms and never uses ease-in. Editorial reveals run 700–1000ms. Banned: parallax, autoplay carousels, marquees, Lenis or scroll-jacking, bouncy springs, confetti, countdowns, load-time popups. Remove html{scroll-behavior:smooth} and the spin-slow, shimmer and marquee keyframes.
- Tokens in @theme: --ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1); --ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77,0,0.175,1); --ease-drawer: cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1); --dur-press 120ms; --dur-fast 160ms; --dur-base 220ms; --dur-drawer-in 420ms; --dur-drawer-out 260ms; --dur-page 400ms; --dur-reveal 700ms.
- Press: buttons :active scale(.98) and icon buttons scale(.92), 120ms --ease-out.
- Hover (inside @media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)): colour and bg shifts 160ms; underline draw 240ms; card second-image crossfade 450ms; image scale 1→1.03 over 1200ms --ease-out; Quick view bar 220ms; mirror glint 700ms once per hover on mirror-tagged products.
- Hero load: the text lines sit in overflow-hidden spans and rise translateY(105%)→0 over 900ms --ease-in-out with a 90ms stagger. The headline bindi on the italic word scales 0→1 over 260ms after the last line. The LCP image is never clipped, delayed or faded.
- Scroll reveals in pure CSS: @supports (animation-timeline: view()) { @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference) { .reveal { animation: fade-up linear both; animation-timeline: view(); animation-range: entry 0% cover 25%; } } } with keyframes opacity 0→1 and translateY 12px→0. Where unsupported, content simply shows. Grid children use --i * 60ms (capped at 8) in a one-time IntersectionObserver-added class. Only section headers, grids and editorial images reveal, never paragraphs.

## Imagery

This is what makes or breaks 'premium', because the neutral UI exposes every inconsistency. (1) ONE BACKDROP: shoot every catalogue piece on a matte limewash/greige board colour-matched by eye to the plinth #EFEAE2 (a painted MDF board or greige card as a seamless sweep). No bedsheets, floors, tiles or pure white. (2) LIGHT: soft north-window daylight, no flash, white foam-board fill on the shadow side, the same time of day, and white balance locked on a grey card at about 5200K. Colour-check each photo against the real piece so teal, mustard and wine stay true. Lippan mirrors should show controlled sparkle, not blown highlights. Use no filters, presets or saturation boosts. (3) SHOTS per product, all 4:5, exported at 1600×2000 WebP/AVIF through next/image: Hero (straight-on, centred, about 8% breathing room; round pieces dead-centre so object-contain never crops them); Detail macro (mirror work, brushwork, resin depth, knot texture, with raking side light; this is also the hover image); In situ with a scale cue (a hand, a switchboard, a lamp, a 4 ft wall, a pooja table), which is the PDP scale shot with the ruler overlay; and an optional Back (signature and date). (4) CUT-OUTS on white get mix-blend-mode:multiply on plinth, except white or cream pieces. Never put an object on a coloured gradient. (5) EDITORIAL: documentary photos of Kavita's real hands, clay, mirrors, brushes, sun-drying and the studio table, in warm natural light, slightly desaturated and never shifting product colour, at 3:2 or 4:5. No stock 'artisan' photos. Used in The Making, Meet Kavita and Behind this piece. (6) HERO: the single best piece in situ on a limewash wall, cropped to 4:5 with the arch in mind (keep the subject in the lower two-thirds so the arch top frames wall, not product). (7) CATEGORY TILES: the best hero shot of each craft on the same plinth, never text or gradients. (8) UGC: real customer homes, 1:1, colours as shot, credited with city. (9) DEMO/PLACEHOLDER until real shots exist: a plinth block with a centred 48px monoline craft icon in line-strong and the craft name as an 11px uppercase muted label, so the grid still looks curated. Never mix random stock. (10) Every image gets descriptive alt text ('Teal Lippan mirror plate, 24 inch, on a limewash wall'). The hero uses priority and fetchPriority='high'; everything else lazy-loads with a plinth-tinted blurDataURL and an explicit sizes attribute.

## Project-specific implementation notes (override the spec where they conflict)

- Never fabricate social proof or craft facts: no invented ratings, '1,200+ homes', 'Google 4.9', mirror counts or hours. Show real numbers from the database (approved reviews, product fields) or omit the element.
- Show 'Cash on delivery' only when `settings.codAvailable` is true. Trust claims must match settings/policies (shipping fee & free threshold, return policy).
- No product variants (size/finish chips) exist in the data model; skip option chips. Personalisation uses the existing `customizable` flag + note field.
- No Instagram feed API: show a simple 'Follow on Instagram' link only when `settings.instagram` is set; never fill an Instagram grid with product photos.
- Keep `<img>` + `imgUrl()` (Cloudinary resizing) rather than next/image; use `loading='lazy'` / `decoding='async'` and `fetchPriority='high'` on the LCP image.
- Kavita's handwritten signature is not available: use her name in Fraunces italic instead of an SVG signature.
- Admin dark mode, command palette and pincode delivery-date promises are out of scope for now (no data to back a delivery date).
